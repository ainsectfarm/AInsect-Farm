Executive summary

400 kW. Realistically, transparently, no spin.

AInsekt Farm is a project to build an integrated circular bioeconomy platform in Poland: a 400 kW biogas plant in high-efficiency cogeneration (Phase I), with optional extensions — a Black Soldier Fly insect farm (Phase II) and an AI Compute centre (Phase III). Phase I is designed as a standalone asset — it defends itself economically. Phases II and III are optional add-ons financed from Phase I cash flow and additional grants.

Key validated assumptions Energy price: FIT 95% × 1,025 PLN/MWh = 974 PLN/MWh for agricultural biogas in HEC <500 kW (Ministry of Climate Regulation 08.11.2023, Polish Journal of Laws 2023 item 2440). Substrate: ≥91% from agricultural origin secures qualification as "agricultural biogas" under the FIT scheme. CAPEX €2.8M: midpoint of 2026 industry benchmarks (€2.5–3.5M per expert sources). FEnIKS Part 5 grant: up to 85% in project finance — the project QUALIFIES (internal heat use, <70% to district heating). All side revenue streams treated conservatively (CO₂ credits: 30% of potential in base case).

02
Problem and market window

Asymmetry between capital, regulation and operators.

Three overlapping trends create a roughly 7-year time window (until the FIT scheme expires on 31.12.2035) that no one in Poland is systematically closing today.

Trend 1 · Renewable infrastructure deficit

Poland imports tens of TWh of energy annually. The country has about 400 agricultural biogas plants with combined capacity of ~250 MW (URE 2025). Germany has ~9,500 such plants. Every 400 kW biogas installation adds ~2,452 MWh per year of controllable, weather-independent baseload energy to the grid. For grid operators, this controllability is worth a premium over the FIT tariff alone.

Trend 2 · Substrate stream without a taker

Polish agriculture wastes ~5 million tons of organic waste annually. In 2025, Polish farms reported ~800,000 tons of potatoes with no buyer. Seasonal surpluses of silage, apples and processing waste — most ends up in disposal or rots in the field. For the farmer, disposal cost is positive; for the biogas operator, it is negative (we receive substrate for free + AINS loyalty tokens + digestate offtake). The asymmetry is structural, not cyclical.

Trend 3 · Expiring FIT

The FIT support scheme for biogas plants <500 kW offers 95% of the reference price guaranteed for 15 years from first energy generation. Reference price for agricultural biogas in HEC: 1,025 PLN/MWh (Ministry of Climate Regulation 08.11.2023, JoL 2023 item 2440). Effective FIT: 974 PLN/MWh × 2,452 MWh = €565K/year (FX 4.23). The mechanism expires no later than 31.12.2035. With COD in September 2029, we have 6 years 3 months of buffer beyond the expiry — a safe execution margin.

Why 400 kW (with 199 kW Plan B)

The first AInsekt Farm biogas plant is planned as 400 kW electrical — full scale maximizing EBITDA while maintaining FIT qualification (<500 kW threshold). Detailed rationale in Section 13 "Plan B: 199 kW pilot as fallback". Briefly: 400 kW yields 2.4× the EBITDA at a similar operational structure; the 199 kW pilot remains a reserve strategy if kill criteria force a scale reduction.

Poland does not need new biogas technology. It needs operators who can close substrate, land and grid connection — in that order.
03
Technical solution

Proven technology. Non-obvious substrate mix.

Standard CSTR reactor configuration (Continuous Stirred-Tank Reactor) with mesophilic fermentation at 38–42 °C. Main fermenter 1,500 m³ + post-fermenter 1,200 m³ in series, total HRT (Hydraulic Retention Time) ~35 days with 15 m³ daily substrate inflow. CHP unit 400 kWel / ~460 kWth. Digestate storage tank (2,500 m³, 6-month capacity required by Polish fertilizer regulations).

CHP waste heat (~460 kWth): ~15% used to maintain fermenter temperature, ~85% available for a potential local off-taker (farm, grain dryer, neighbouring plant) or as reserve for the future Phase II BSF facility. The base model does not assume heat revenue — that is upside, not foundation. After a heat off-take agreement: additional €30–50K per year.

04
Substrate supply chain

Logistics is the project. 91% agricultural substrate.

Most failed Polish biogas plants did not fail on technology — they failed on a missing stable substrate stream. Our model for 400 kW: four logistics zones, two own trucks + reserve contract, buffer storage adapted to agricultural seasonality. Critical metric: ≥91% agricultural substrate — secures the "agricultural biogas" qualification in the FIT scheme (reference price 1,025 PLN/MWh).

Operational detail often missed Spent brewery grains contain 75–80% water. One ton of spent grains ≈ 250 kg dry matter. The first biogas designer who ignores this gets 30% less biogas than the CHP supplier's brochure promised. 07
Risk analysis and kill criteria

What can actually go wrong.

Generic risk matrices are useless. Below — real, sector-specific risks for a 400 kW plant in Poland, ordered by probability. Each key risk has an assigned kill criterion.

R.01
Substrate qualification as "agricultural biogas in HEC"
Critical
Reference price 1,025 PLN/MWh applies to "agricultural biogas in HEC". Our mix (91% agricultural + 9% hospitality) requires URE legal interpretation. Mitigation: individual URE interpretation request in M2; safety margin 91% > typical 80% threshold; alternative agricultural suppliers as reserve.
R.02
Grid connection conditions delay (DSO)
Critical
Polish DSOs can issue conditions in 12–24 months. Mitigation: connection application as the FIRST step after SPV setup; two parallel scenarios — MV and LV locations; preselection of plots within <500 m of existing grid infrastructure.
R.03
Environmental Decision required for >250 kW
Medium
DUŚ procedure mandatory for 400 kW (6–12 months). Mitigation: KIP filing to RDOŚ in M2; preselection of locations with ≥500 m buffer to residential zones; public consultation before zoning; Plan B: relocation if needed.
R.04
Insufficient substrate supply in Year 1
Medium
Three-layer mitigation: (1) 3 binding LOIs / framework agreements before FEnIKS, (2) starter stock 800 t silage pre-launch (~€16K), (3) reserve contract with 2 large farms within 30 km for 100% backup supply.
R.05
Public opposition / NIMBY
Medium
Plot selection with ≥500 m buffer to residential zones. Hermetic construction (sealed tanks, biofilter). Public consultation BEFORE zoning. Digestate sold to local farmers = local value narrative.
R.06
CHP failure > 30 days
Medium
All-inclusive service contract with supplier (Jenbacher / MWM) with >95% availability guarantee and penalty clauses. BI insurance for €350K. Plan B: raw gas sale to nearest CHP / gas grid — emergency option.

Kill criteria — when we do not proceed

For a professional investor, the question is not "what will we do to succeed" but "when will we stop". Hard criteria below:

  • K.01 · No grid connection conditions within 12 months → suspend land purchase, relocate.
  • K.02 · No 3 binding agricultural supplier LOIs by M6 → suspend FEnIKS submission, re-segment region.
  • K.03 · Negative URE "agricultural biogas" interpretation by M6 → restructure mix to 95% agricultural (drop hospitality zone). Alternatively: switch to FENX.02.02 (no HEC requirement).
  • K.04 · Negative DUŚ by M9 → relocate; parallel process in new plot.
  • K.05 · EPC quotes > €3.2M → rescope to 299 kW. Plan C: pivot to 199 kW pilot.
  • K.06 · FEnIKS rejection after 2 call cycles → switch to FENX.02.02. Plan C: WFOŚiGW full loan + 299 kW scale.
  • K.07 · Public opposition in consultations → withdraw by M3 max.

Phase II — BSF (brief)

Black Soldier Fly (Hermetia illucens) — 1,200 m² insect facility producing 53 t BSF meal (feed protein) + 22 t larval oil + 900 t frass (premium fertilizer) annually. Uses CHP waste heat (15% internal, 70% for BSF). Regulatory status: approved as animal feed — aquaculture (2017), poultry and pigs (2021), pet food (2023). Human consumption application in progress at EFSA. EBITDA ~€196K per year. Phase payback: 3.4 years.

Phase III — AI Compute (brief)

16-GPU NVIDIA H100/H200 AI Compute centre powered directly by biogas (internal PPA) — "green AI infrastructure" with a 20–40% premium above standard market price. Uses GPU waste heat (~150 kWth) back into BSF or digestate drying. Status: call option requiring 2030 market validation. EBITDA ~€462K per year. Phase payback: 2.8 years.

Sequence logic Each step is conditional on the previous succeeding — not automatic. The second biogas module only after 15 months of stable Phase I operations. Phase II BSF only after two successful biogas modules. Phase III AI Compute only if the 2031 green compute market sustains 20–40% premiums. Phase I alone is a profitable project — subsequent phases are asymmetric call options whose failure does not threaten Phase I returns.
